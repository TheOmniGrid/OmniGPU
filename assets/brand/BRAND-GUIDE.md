# OmniGPU brand guide

## Brand position

OmniGPU is the gaming graphics control product in the Omnivex tool family. It should feel precise, capable and calm: enthusiast-grade control without noisy “gamer” clutter or unsupported promises.

Core line: **One GPU control center. Built for gaming.**

Supporting promise: **Capability-driven control for NVIDIA, AMD and Intel.**

## Voice

- Direct and technically credible.
- Gamer-focused, not developer/lab-focused.
- Confident without claiming universal compatibility or risk-free tuning.
- Plain language first; exact technical boundary immediately available.
- Safety phrased as control and transparency, not fear.

Use: “reviewed installation,” “supported by the installed driver,” “optional community support,” “fails closed.”
Avoid: “one-click magic,” “works on every GPU,” “zero risk,” “official vendor app,” “all features guaranteed.”

## Typography

- Display/product name: Bahnschrift SemiBold/Bold, uppercase where the existing identity uses it.
- Interface and body: Segoe UI, regular/semibold.
- Data/technical values: Bahnschrift or tabular figures.
- Public body copy: 16 px minimum equivalent, 1.5 line-height, 60–75 character measure.
- Use a clear scale such as 14/16/18/24/32/48/64; avoid microcopy below 12 px.

## Icon and logo rules

- Use the supplied OmniGPU icon without changing proportions, graphics-card/cooling-turbine geometry, internal cyan-to-blue-to-violet-to-purple balance or corner treatment.
- The GPU card outline, fan and compute lines are one indivisible center mark; do not recolor individual segments, add a performance arrow, reuse OmniScale's display motif or restore the retired chip/bar-chart mark.
- Use the approved complete tile exactly as supplied; do not reconstruct or independently rescale its GPU card, fan or circular-disc relationship.
- The complete tile uses one upper-left diagonal groove with one violet accent; do not add a second short seam or connector line touching the circular rim.
- The complete tile must finish in a clean dark-graphite perimeter. Do not add a white/silver outline, bright bevel, exterior glow, drop shadow or pale alpha fringe.
- The dedicated Setup/MSI export uses genuine transparency and only a 2–3% antialiased safety margin so Windows does not show a heavy black outer band.
- Use the standalone transparent GPU/fan symbol in every in-app product-identity surface, including the title bar and Settings/About card. Use the complete dark tile for the Windows executable, MSI/Add-or-Remove-Programs identity, `Setup.exe`, store listings and external product artwork.
- Keep clear space equal to at least 20% of the icon width.
- Do not place the icon on busy imagery without a dark quiet zone.
- Vendor marks are identification only; do not combine them into the OmniGPU logo or imply endorsement.
- Use one consistent line-icon family for interface/support graphics; no emoji as structural icons.

## Photography and generated art

- Public product proof uses real application captures only.
- The abstract GPU-core background is decorative marketing art, not a UI screenshot or hardware claim.
- Do not fabricate benchmark results, vendor controls, review quotes or download counts.
- Prefer matte black technical texture, precise circuit geometry and controlled edge light.
- Avoid rainbow RGB, excessive bloom, aggressive cyberpunk clutter and generic esports flames.

## Layout

- Use 4/8 px spacing rhythm and strong alignment.
- One primary action/claim per surface.
- Group features by player goal: maintain, profile, measure, tune, display.
- Use thin 1–2 px borders and tonal surfaces rather than heavy shadows.
- Keep critical cover content in the central 70–80%; Patreon’s current header places the key visual toward the right and avoids embedded text.

## Motion

Marketing animations, if later created, should be subtle and functional: 120–180 ms controls, 220–300 ms panel transitions, transform/opacity only, interruptible and disabled for reduced-motion users. Animate at most one or two focal layers.

## Screenshot standards

- Capture the actual current build at a consistent 1440×900 window.
- Use real hardware values and label them as examples.
- Remove usernames, absolute paths, receipts, account names and unrelated desktop content.
- Keep the full app frame unless a clearly disclosed crop improves readability.
- Never retouch functionality into a screenshot.
- Naming: `omnigpu-<version>-<page>.png`.
