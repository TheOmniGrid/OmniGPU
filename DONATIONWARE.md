# Donationware

OmniGPU is donationware:

- free to use, with no required payment;
- no advertisements or paid feature tier;
- no source code, installer or binary is distributed through this GitHub repository;
- optional support is accepted through the official Ko-fi and Patreon pages;
- donations fund development, documentation, compatibility work and real-hardware validation.

A contribution is voluntary. It does not buy ownership of OmniGPU, source-code access, redistribution rights, resale rights, trademark rights or priority over non-donating users.

## Official support pages

- Ko-fi: <https://ko-fi.com/theomnigrid>
- Patreon: <https://www.patreon.com/TheOmniGrid>

If you cannot or do not want to contribute, that does not reduce the application's feature set. Please help by reporting reproducible compatibility problems, correcting documentation or directing users away from unofficial mirrors.

## Recommended public wording

> OmniGPU is free donationware with no ads and no paid feature tier. Optional Ko-fi or Patreon support helps fund hardware validation, compatibility work and continued development.

Do not describe OmniGPU as risk-free, officially endorsed by GPU vendors, guaranteed to expose every control on every GPU, or available through unofficial mirrors.
