# Public roadmap

The product version remains **1.0.0** until an explicit version decision is made. Roadmap items are goals, not purchase guarantees.

## Release completion

- Production Authenticode signing and post-signing verification inventory.
- Disposable clean-system delivery/lifecycle validation.
- Controlled NVIDIA/AMD/Intel write/read-back hardware matrix.
- Multi-display HDR, resolution/refresh and DDC/CI certification matrix.
- Final jurisdiction-specific legal, privacy, refund and tax review.

## Compatibility maintenance

- Track new NVIDIA, AMD and Intel driver branches through official APIs.
- Expand real-hardware coverage without fixed GPU-generation allowlists.
- Maintain NVIDIA profile metadata and portable import/export compatibility.
- Keep Windows Update, PresentMon, Game Bar and local store discovery paths current.

## Player experience

- Improve plain-language recovery guidance and compatibility reporting.
- Expand safe per-game presets only where official driver contracts exist.
- Continue performance, accessibility and five-language quality passes.
- Evaluate additional display/gaming features only when documented APIs and rollback validation are available.

No automatic OC scanner, unsupported NVIDIA fan curve or undocumented driver mutation is promised by this roadmap.
