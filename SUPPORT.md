# Support

## Before contacting support

Include:

- OmniGPU version and Windows version;
- GPU model, laptop/desktop and current driver version;
- exact page/action involved;
- what you expected and what happened;
- whether Windows, the driver or OmniGPU was restarted;
- a screenshot with usernames, paths, license details and unrelated personal data removed.

Do not post operation plans, private download links, purchase receipts, full logs containing local paths or suspected security vulnerabilities in a public issue.

## Contact routes

- General support: `omnivex@theomnigrid.biz`
- Public documentation/issues: <https://github.com/TheOmniGrid>
- Patreon access/payment questions: <https://www.patreon.com/TheOmniGrid>
- Ko-fi access/payment questions: <https://ko-fi.com/theomnigrid>
- Security reports: private GitHub security advisory or `omnivex@theomnigrid.biz`

## Support scope

Support covers authorized, unmodified OmniGPU builds on Windows 10/11 x64. Modified packages, mirrors, cracked copies, unsupported driver modifications, guessed voltage/clock ranges and damage caused by bypassing safety controls are outside support scope. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
